# Provenance

I built this repository from the reviewed VOLLEY working-tree snapshot dated 2026-08-03,
derived from base commit `d82877a`. The snapshot includes numerical corrections that were
not yet committed in the source checkout. I therefore identify it by per-file SHA-256 hashes
and the aggregate `snapshot_sha256` in `SOURCE_MANIFEST.json`, not by inventing a commit.

The retained files establish what was computed. They do not establish that a motor was built,
that a field or force was measured, or that any external organization endorsed the result.
